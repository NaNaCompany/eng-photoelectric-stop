Photoelectric Stopping Voltage Calculator

Calculate the reverse voltage magnitude needed to stop the fastest photoelectrons in a single-photon model. Choose input units and calculate one value within the stated conditions.

Inputs

Greater than zero after conversion to SI

Zero or greater

Calculations run in this browser. Scientific notation such as 1.2e-3 is supported. Do not group thousands.

Result

Stopping voltage magnitude V_stop

—

Enter values and select Calculate.

FORMULA

V_stop = (hc/λ − φ)/e, when hc/λ ≥ φ

Assumptions

Required: λ>0 and φ≥0. Supply the work function of the actual surface. Use monochromatic single-photon excitation. Excluded: multiphoton processes, thermionic emission, contact potentials and surface contamination. Below threshold the app reports no emission; at threshold V_stop=0. The result is a reverse-voltage magnitude and does not predict photocurrent or quantum efficiency.

Displayed values are floating-point approximations. Design safety factors and unprovided error contributions are not calculated.
